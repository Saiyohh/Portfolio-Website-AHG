Planet Glasidea

Level Designer, solo project 2023

  • UE5.2
  • Level Test
  • Destiny 2
  • Combat-former

What I did

  • Designed a Destiny-style patrol zone: an open area whose landmarks funnel players toward encounters.
  • Directed flow with sight lines, elevation changes, and environmental cues instead of hard barriers.
  • Calibrated cover and engagement distances for FPS combat, with multiple approach angles in each arena.

The level

Planet Glasidea is an FPS level design study built in Unreal Engine 5, inspired by the environmental storytelling and encounter design of Destiny. It explores how a sci-fi environment can guide players through combat spaces while keeping a sense of wonder and discovery on an alien world.

The open area encourages exploration while keeping players on a readable critical path. Each arena offers fallback positions as well as approach angles, giving players tactical options within the space.

Process

The level began as a top-down sketch focused on flow and pacing, then moved into Unreal for blockout. Iteration focused on the relationship between traversal spaces (low intensity, scenic) and combat arenas (high intensity, tactical), so the level never felt like a series of disconnected rooms.

Takeaways

Destiny's levels work because they balance spectacle with function: every vista doubles as a waypoint, every canyon doubles as a funnel. Recreating that philosophy from scratch deepened my understanding of how FPS spaces are composed.
