  • Developed the worldbuilding, story structure, and cinematic direction.

The game

KillCode: 404 is a fast-paced FPS roguelike set inside a fractured virtual world known as the Pantheon. Players step into the role of Kasey, a rogue netrunner hacking her way through hostile cyberspace to uncover the secrets buried beneath its corporate architecture.

It draws on the movement of Titanfall, the roguelike structure of RoboQuest, and the digital meta-absurdism of Ready Player One. Combat revolves around weapons inspired by Greek mythology: the Zeus atomic cannon, the Artemis lever-action shotgun, the Poseidon sniper rifle, and the Decompiler auto-shotgun, each with a distinct playstyle that interacts with the movement systems.

Level design

Development

KillCode: 404 was developed by Studio 300 over the course of 2025 as our flagship title. The team of 13 included artists, programmers, a combat designer, concept artists, a sound designer, and a music composer. I led design across all disciplines and was responsible for maintaining the creative vision throughout production.

It won Best in Show for Overall Design at the Academy of Art University 2025 Spring Show. The demo is available for download on itch.io.
