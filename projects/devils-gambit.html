What I did

  • Defined the game's core identity: worldbuilding, player abilities, combat systems, and overall level flow.
  • Programmed the player character controller and the boss encounter in C#.
  • Wrote the dialogue and narrative cutscenes.
  • Created promotional assets, including logos, character art direction, and marketing materials.

The game

Devil's Gambit is a 2D dark-fantasy Metroidvania that casts players as the Charlatan, a doomed gambler bound to a devilish patron. Players wield cursed playing cards and hellish magic to cheat fate, challenge Hell's hierarchy, and fight their way out of its decaying, layered depths.

It was developed as a vertical slice in Unity by a small team at Studio 300. The entire demo was concepted, designed, built, and polished in a 59-day production window.

Development

Development moved fast with a tight scope. Early production focused on locking the core combat loop and movement feel, while the art team established the hand-drawn visual style in parallel.

Combat and boss

Level design

Level design went through rapid iteration, from paper sketches to blockouts to final tilesets, with the boss encounter serving as the centerpiece of the vertical slice.

Results and takeaways

Devil's Gambit was selected as a finalist at the Academy of Art University 2024 Spring Show. The tight timeline taught the team how to scope aggressively and prioritize the features that would make the strongest impression in a vertical slice.
